# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Jixel to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
experience, background or identity. We pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, including in disagreement;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the users and the community;
- helping newcomers, and remembering everyone was one once.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks;
- harassment, public or private; unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
chats — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers via the contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (mark the message "Code of Conduct"). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions
and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code.
